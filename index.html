I'm a video editor and graphic designer from Jaipur. My main work is in editing — promotional reels, cinematic personal projects, podcast clips, and social-first content. I care about pacing, colour, and making sure a video feels right from the first second to the last.

I've worked with real clients — a gym launch, a Dubai property brand, an AI marketing agency, a podcast — and I bring the same attention to every cut whether it's a 15-second reel or a 3-minute promo.

I also draw and make art reels, which shows up in my personal YouTube work. But video editing is what I do for clients, and it's where I want to grow.
